[Ek Melon](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/ek-melon/id6761439309) does one thing: you take a photo of a watermelon and it tells you whether the melon is ripe.

## The pipeline

The app is built with React Native and Supabase. A photo goes to Gemini Vision, which scores four visual dimensions. The app turns those scores into:

- a ripeness verdict with a **confidence** score
- a **plain-language breakdown** of why
- all of it in **4+ Indian languages**

## Closing the loop

A vision model is only as good as its feedback. The app records what users find out after cutting the melon, which builds up a labelled dataset over time.

## Solo, end to end

I did the design, the pipeline, the app and the App Store release myself. It's rated **5.0** on the App Store.
